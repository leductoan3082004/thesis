\section{Summary}
\label{sec:summary}

SHDFL unifies decentralized topology design, secure aggregation, and blockchain-anchored coordination into a hierarchical architecture suited for cross-silo deployments. Balanced clusters produced by Dirichlet-guided D-clique construction feed a timer-driven state/nation hierarchy in which External Cluster Models flow upward through bridge fan-out and scope checkpoints propagate downward via asynchronous pulls. Embedding the Bonawitz Secure Aggregation Procedure inside every cluster round ensures that only quorum-protected aggregates ever leave a cluster, and the restart semantics documented in Section~\ref{sec:secure-aggregation-sap} guarantee privacy even amid repeated dropouts. Experiments on CIFAR-10 show convergence under strong label skew, resilience through Round Amplification, and the bandwidth trade-offs between SAP and plaintext aggregation, confirming that hierarchical coordination plus SAP delivers practical privacy, auditability, and scalability for decentralized FL.

Looking ahead, two enhancements are most pressing. First, SAP’s masking inflates payloads, so we will explore compression schemes tailored to masked gradients to retain privacy while reducing bandwidth. Second, static cluster assignments can strand a cluster when members fail permanently; we therefore plan to design mid-run rebalancing mechanisms that detect underpopulated clusters and safely redistribute surviving nodes, allowing the hierarchy to maintain quorum without manual intervention.
