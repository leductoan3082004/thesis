\begin{abstract}
We present SHDFL, a secure hierarchical decentralized federated learning framework that unifies topology-aware clustering, the Bonawitz secure aggregation protocol, and blockchain-anchored coordination. Nodes are organized into label-balanced clusters via D-clique construction; each cluster executes the Secure Aggregation Procedure so only quorum-protected updates leave the clique, bridge nodes gossip External Cluster Models to neighboring clusters, and designated fan-out members dispatch confirmed scope checkpoints to the timer-driven state/nation hierarchy. Scope checkpoints are published through IPFS with ledger anchors, enabling asynchronous pull-based dissemination without blocking local training. This integration delivers privacy guarantees comparable to centralized secure aggregation while preserving the scalability of decentralized overlays. Experiments on CIFAR-10 with 24 nodes—covering transient dropout campaigns, SAP-free ablations, and communication profiling—show that SHDFL maintains accuracy under severe non-IID skew, guarantees privacy through round restarts, and quantifies the bandwidth trade-offs introduced by masking. The results demonstrate that hierarchical coordination plus SAP delivers practical cross-silo FL with auditable storage and robust convergence.
\end{abstract}
